## Formal clearance package required

Likely relevant Nice classes must be finalized with counsel, expected to include software/SaaS and business/technology services (commonly 9, 35 and 42 depending on the final specification). Search exact, begins-with, fuzzy, phonetic and conceptually similar marks in:

- Austrian Patent Office see.ip
- EUIPO eSearch/TMview
- WIPO Global Brand Database / Madrid
- USPTO
- UK IPO
- priority launch jurisdictions and company registers

Also review domains, major app stores and social handles. For the final figurative mark, perform image-similarity/design searches after the artwork exists.

## Production naming invariant

Until legal clearance is signed off, source code and design prototypes use a central brand token. No package names, database identifiers, protocol schema IDs or public URLs are renamed to a candidate brand.
